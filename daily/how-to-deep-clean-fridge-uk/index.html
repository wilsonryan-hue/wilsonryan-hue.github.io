How to deep clean the fridge — UK monthly reset

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Fridge smells are old sauce and wet drawers. Empty, wash, date-check.

Eight moves. Once a month.

  1. Cooler bag ready. Park perishables while you work.
  2. Everything out. See the real inventory. Bin expired without debate.
  3. Drawers in the sink. Hot soapy water. Dry fully before replace.
  4. Shelves wipe. Bicarb on stubborn sticky rings.
  5. Check seals. Wipe the rubber gasket. Crumbs cause seal fails.
  6. Restock by zone. Leftovers front, raw meat bottom, sauces door.
  7. Open-box dates. Marker on sauces. 30-day rule for most.
  8. Meal grid after. Free meal grid uses what you kept.
