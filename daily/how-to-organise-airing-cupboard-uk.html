How to organise an airing cupboard — UK linen system

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Airing cupboards fail when sheets are unfolded and towels are mixed by size.

Seven moves. One cupboard. Calm mornings.

  1. Empty completely. You cannot organise what you cannot see.
  2. Match sets. Sheet + duvet + pillowcases together with a ribbon or in one pillowcase.
  3. Towels by size. Bath, hand, tea. Same fold every time.
  4. Donate excess. Two sets per bed is enough. Charity shop the rest.
  5. Heavier down low. Spare duvets bottom. Daily towels at eye level.
  6. Label shelves. Masking tape. Guests and teens can help without wrecking it.
  7. Life admin note. Add linen audit quarterly to the Life Admin Checklist.
