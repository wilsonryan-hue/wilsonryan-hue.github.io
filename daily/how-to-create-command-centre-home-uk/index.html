24 September 2026 · 5 min

Lost keys and school letters are a wall problem, not a personality problem.

Eight moves. One wall by the door.

  1. Pick the door wall. Where bags land. Not the pretty hallway nobody uses.
  2. Key hooks at adult height. One hook per person. Names optional.
  3. Wall calendar. Paper beats five apps for family visibility.
  4. Post tray. In / action / recycle. Clear weekly.
  5. Charger dock. One strip. Phones charge here.
  6. School letter clip. Permission slips live here until signed.
  7. Weekly reset. Sunday: clear tray, update calendar.
  8. Checklist backup. Life Admin Checklist covers the weekly clear.
