How to use active recall flashcards — UK revision system

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Flashcards fail when they are copied notes. Question on front, answer on back.

Seven moves. Cards that force recall.

  1. One fact per card. Not a paragraph. One question.
  2. Own words on back. If you cannot say it simply, you do not know it.
  3. Spaced pile. Daily / every 3 days / weekly. Move cards when earned.
  4. Say answers out loud. Silent reading lies.
  5. Cull easy cards. Do not waste sessions on what you already own.
  6. Paper or app. Either works. Consistency beats brand.
  7. Sunday review pile. Pair this with the Revision Timetable so subjects get booked hours.
