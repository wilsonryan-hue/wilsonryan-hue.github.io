How to reduce household clutter — UK one-in-one-out

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Clutter is unfinished decisions. One-in-one-out and a donation bag by the door.

Seven moves. Steady reduction.

  1. Donation bag by the door. Always open. Drop as you decide.
  2. One-in-one-out. New jumper in, old jumper out. Same day.
  3. Flat surfaces policy. Counters and dining table are not storage.
  4. Ten-minute tidy nightly. Timer. Whole household. Music optional.
  5. No new storage yet. Organise less stuff first. Boxes hide problems.
  6. Sentimental box limit. One box per adult. Photo the rest.
  7. Sunday review. Sunday Reset Workbook includes a clutter ten-minute block.
