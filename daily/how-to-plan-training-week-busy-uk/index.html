How to plan a training week when life is busy — UK

24 September 2026 · 5 min

A training week fails when it assumes empty evenings. Plan the real calendar first, then put sessions where they fit.

Eight moves for a week you can actually finish.

  1. Open the week. Write work late nights, school runs, and one non-negotiable rest evening.
  2. Pick the goal. Strength, fitness, or showing up — choose one primary for 7 days.
  3. Count sessions. Three quality sessions beat five half-done ones. Cap at what the calendar allows.
  4. Assign days. Put hard sessions on lighter life days. Easy sessions beside late finishes.
  5. Time-box. 45–60 minutes door-to-door. Write start time, not “after work”.
  6. Prep the night before. Kit out, bottle filled, session written on one line.
  7. Track done, not perfect. Tick the session. Missed days get moved once — then drop.
  8. Printable. A Weekly Training Plan keeps the undated week, session types, and recovery note in one PDF.
