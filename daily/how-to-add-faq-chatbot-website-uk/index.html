How to add an FAQ chatbot to your website — UK small business

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Chatbots fail when nobody writes the FAQs. Content first, widget second.

Eight moves. Answers before automation.

  1. List the ten questions you answer weekly. Price, hours, delivery, refunds, location.
  2. Write short answers. UK English. Link to the real policy page.
  3. Choose tool that fits your stack. Many site builders have native bots. Do not overbuy.
  4. Tone equals your brand. Not corporate American. Not slang soup.
  5. Handoff to human. Email or form when the bot is stuck. Always.
  6. Test on mobile. Most visitors are on phones.
  7. Review transcripts monthly. New FAQs come from real misses.
  8. DFY option. DFY Site Chatbot Setup if you want it built for you.
