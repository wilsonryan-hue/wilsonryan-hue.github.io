How to run a laundry system on a busy UK week

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Laundry fails at the folding step. Wash small, hang same day, put away same day.

Seven moves. No chair-drobe.

  1. Two-load maximum per day. More creates folding debt.
  2. Wash while you cook. Parallel chores. Idle machine time is waste.
  3. Hang or tumble immediately. Wet laundry in the machine overnight smells.
  4. Fold at the machine. Walking to the sofa with a mountain guarantees a pile.
  5. Put away before bed. Clean laundry on the chair is dirty laundry cosplay.
  6. Uniform hangers. Sounds fussy. Speeds every morning.
  7. Admin the kit. Life Admin Checklist includes detergent stock and filter cleans.
