How to write client proposals with AI — UK freelancer

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Generic AI proposals lose. Specific scope, price, and timeline win.

Eight moves. Specific beats shiny.

  1. Paste the brief and constraints. Budget, deadline, must-haves.
  2. Structure fixed. Problem, approach, deliverables, timeline, price, next step.
  3. AI drafts sections. You rewrite anything vague.
  4. Numbers you own. Days, fees, deposit. AI does not price your life.
  5. Risk and assumptions. Say what is out of scope. Prevents scope creep.
  6. One-page option. Busy clients skim. Offer a short version.
  7. Proofread names. Wrong company name kills trust instantly.
