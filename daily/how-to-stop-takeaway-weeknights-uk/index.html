How to stop weeknight takeaways — UK 5pm rescue plan

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Takeaway is not a treat when it is the default. Keep a freezer backup that beats the app.

Seven moves. Beat the delivery fee.

  1. Name the trigger. Usually hunger plus decision fatigue at 5pm. Not laziness.
  2. Backup tea list. Three freezer meals you actually like. Not sad diet food.
  3. App off home Wi-Fi. Delete or log out of delivery apps on the house tablet.
  4. 20-minute rule. If dinner can be hot in 20, the app loses.
  5. Shop the backups. Frozen gyoza, fish fingers, tortellini. Own brand.
  6. Friday treat slot. One planned takeaway. Guilt-free. Rest of week protected.
  7. Printable teas. School-Night Teas Under 30 removes the 5pm decision.
