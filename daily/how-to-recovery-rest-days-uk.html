How to use rest days without losing progress — UK

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Rest is part of the plan. Treat it like a session so the next hard day still happens.

Eight rules for recovery that keep the week on track.

  1. Schedule rest. Mark at least one full rest day before the week starts.
  2. Define rest. No hard sets. Walking and light mobility count as recovery, not failure.
  3. Sleep first. Protect bedtime the night before a hard session — it beats another coffee.
  4. Food simple. Protein + carbs on rest days too. Under-eating makes the next session worse.
  5. Soreness check. Sharp pain = stop. Dull ache = light walk. Guessing is not a plan.
  6. Write the next session. Rest day ends with tomorrow’s workout on paper — one line is enough.
  7. Avoid the slide. One rest day stays one. Do not renegotiate the whole week.
  8. Printable. The Weekly Training Plan has a recovery note so rest is planned, not improvised.
