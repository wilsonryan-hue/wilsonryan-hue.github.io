How to build an evening tidy habit — UK 10-minute reset

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Morning stress is often last night's mess. Ten minutes before bed changes the tone.

Seven moves. Every night.

  1. Same trigger. After the kettle for bedtime tea. Habit stacked.
  2. Timer 10:00. Stop when it rings.
  3. Kitchen first. Sink clear, counters wipe.
  4. Living room surfaces. Remote, cups, toys to homes.
  5. Bags packed. Work bag, school bag, keys on hook.
  6. Clothes for morning. One decision removed at 7am.
  7. Free Sunday Reset. Weekly deeper version on the free page.
