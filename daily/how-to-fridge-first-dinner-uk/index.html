How to cook fridge-first dinners — UK no-waste method

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Shopping first wastes food. Open the fridge, then write the list for the gaps only.

Seven moves. Fridge before trolley.

  1. Empty the middle shelf. Lay it on the counter. See what is real, not what you remember.
  2. Group by cook style. Fry, roast, boil, eat-cold. Match pairs.
  3. One hero + sides. Protein or leftover becomes the hero. Veg and carb fill the plate.
  4. Gap list only. Milk, bread, one missing spice. Nothing else.
  5. Cook the oldest first. Dates face forward. Tonight eats yesterday's risk.
  6. Freeze or bin rule. If you will not eat it in 48 hours, decide now.
  7. Grid the week. Use the free meal grid so fridge-first becomes habit.
