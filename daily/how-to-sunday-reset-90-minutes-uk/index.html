How to do a Sunday reset in 90 minutes — UK checklist

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Sunday dread is usually undecided work. Ninety minutes with a list beats an evening of half-jobs.

Set a timer. When it ends, you stop. Unfinished items roll to next Sunday.

  1. Timer on. 90 minutes. Stop when it ends.
  2. Kitchen. Empty sink, wipe counters, dishwasher or wash the pile, clear the table.
  3. Clothes. One laundry cycle on. Put away one basket. Not the whole wardrobe.
  4. Calendar. Next week: three fixed appointments and one grocery slot.
  5. Bags. School or work bags packed. Keys and chargers in one place.
  6. Fridge. Bin expired. Note three dinners you already have ingredients for.
  7. Shop list. From fridge gaps, not from Instagram recipes.
  8. Fence it. Use the free Sunday Reset PDF, or the paid reset workbook for the full week page.
