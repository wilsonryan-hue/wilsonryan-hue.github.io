How to budget on UK payday — before the money leaves

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Payday budgeting fails when you plan after the first spend. Do the split the morning money lands.

Eight moves. Same morning as pay.

  1. Open banking. Write the exact amount that arrived — not what you hoped.
  2. Fixed bills. Rent or mortgage, council tax, utilities, broadband, debt minimums due before next payday.
  3. Subtract. Pay minus fixed bills = working float. Not free money.
  4. Assign the float. Groceries, travel, kids, small buffer. Numbers on paper beat vibes.
  5. Move bill money. Bills pot or separate account the same day if you can.
  6. One sinking fund. Car, Christmas, boiler — even £10 on payday.
  7. Debt fire. Snowball or avalanche. Pick one method for 30 days.
  8. Printable. The UK Payday Budget Pack is this split as a working PDF.
