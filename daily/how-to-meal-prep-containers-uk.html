How to meal prep with containers — UK lunchbox system

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Meal prep dies when containers leak, smell, or look sad by Wednesday. System beats motivation.

Eight moves. Prep once. Eat all week.

  1. Match lids first. Orphan lids go in the donate bag. Matched sets only.
  2. Glass for wet. Curries and sauces in glass. Plastic for dry carbs and snacks.
  3. Cook cool pack. Food cools 20 minutes before lids. Stops soggy condensation.
  4. Label day + dish. Masking tape. Mon lunch, Tue dinner. No mystery boxes.
  5. Stack by day. Front to back Mon to Fri. Grab without thinking.
  6. Sauce separate. Dressings in small pots. Salad stays crisp.
  7. One free night. Friday is leftover roulette or takeaway. Built-in mercy.
  8. Tea plan PDF. School-Night Teas pairs with this container system.
