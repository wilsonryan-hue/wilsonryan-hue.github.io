How to build a morning run habit — UK dark mornings

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Morning runs die in October. Kit by the door and a tiny minimum beat motivation.

Seven moves. Dark mornings included.

  1. Kit stacked night before. Kit by the door. Shoes unlaced ready.
  2. Ten-minute minimum. If you hate it after ten, walk home. Usually you continue.
  3. Head torch + reflective. Not optional from October. Be seen.
  4. Same loop. Decision fatigue is the enemy. One route for 30 days.
  5. Coffee after, not before. Run first. Reward after. Order matters.
  6. Missed day protocol. Next morning, not I will do evening instead (you will not).
  7. Sunday check-in. Pair this with the Weekly Training Plan so sessions stay on the calendar.
