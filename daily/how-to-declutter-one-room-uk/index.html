How to declutter one room in an afternoon — UK method

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Decluttering fails when you buy boxes first. Decide, then contain what remains.

Eight moves. One room. One afternoon.

  1. Define done. Floor clear, surfaces clear, one donation bag. Photo optional.
  2. Three piles. Keep, donate, bin. No maybe pile — maybe is keep with guilt.
  3. Start at the door. Work clockwise. Do not hop to the hardest drawer first.
  4. Off-floor first. Clothes and toys off the carpet before sentimental drawers.
  5. One keep criterion. Used in 12 months or truly loved. Not might-need.
  6. Bag leave same day. Car boot or hallway. Donation that sits becomes clutter again.
  7. Wipe and replace. Clean shelf, then put keepers back. Feels finished.
  8. Admin the rest. Life Admin Checklist catches the paperwork pile that always migrates.
