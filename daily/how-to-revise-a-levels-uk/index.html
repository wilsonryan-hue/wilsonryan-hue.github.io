How to revise for A-levels — UK sixth-form plan

24 September 2026 · 5 min

A-levels punish shallow notes. Depth on weak modules beats covering everything lightly.

Eight moves. Depth over theatre.

  1. Specification open. Print the exam board spec. Tick what you can teach a friend.
  2. Essay subjects: plans first. Structure before prose. Markers reward structure.
  3. STEM: problem sets. Do questions until the method is boring.
  4. Teacher office hours. Book them with a specific question. Not I am confused.
  5. Two subjects per day max. Context switching wastes hours.
  6. Sleep before all-nighters. Memory consolidates when you sleep. All-nighters are cosplay.
  7. Mock autopsy. Every mock: error log within 48 hours.
  8. Clear life admin. Life Admin Checklist stops form-filling from stealing revision.
