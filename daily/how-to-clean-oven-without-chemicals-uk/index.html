How to clean the oven without harsh chemicals — UK method

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Harsh oven sprays work but punish lungs. Bicarb paste overnight does the job.

Seven moves. Overnight paste.

  1. Cool oven only. Never clean hot.
  2. Racks out. Soak in hot soapy water in the sink or bath.
  3. Bicarb paste. Bicarb + water. Coat greasy walls. Avoid heating elements if exposed.
  4. Overnight wait. Patience is the product.
  5. Wipe and vinegar spray. Vinegar fizz lifts residue. Microfibre wipe.
  6. Racks scrub and dry. Back in clean.
  7. Schedule quarterly. Sunday Reset Workbook reminds you before it becomes archaeology.
