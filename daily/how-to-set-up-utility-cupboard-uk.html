How to set up a utility cupboard — UK cleaning station

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Cleaning stalls when kit is scattered. One cupboard, labelled, restocked.

Seven moves. One station.

  1. All cleaning kit in one place. Stop hunting under sinks in three rooms.
  2. Shelf by job. Bathroom, kitchen, floors. Not by brand.
  3. Cloth colour code. Kitchen cloths never touch the loo. Non-negotiable.
  4. Stock list inside door. Tick when low. Add to shop.
  5. Hazard high or locked. If kids or pets. Legal common sense.
  6. Vacuum filters cleaned. Monthly. Performance depends on it.
  7. Reset workbook. Sunday Reset Workbook schedules the weekly restock glance.
