How to talk about money as a couple — UK payday meeting

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Money talks fail when they are late-night blame sessions. Payday, agenda, timer.

Seven moves. Thirty minutes. No shouting.

  1. Same payday slot. Calendar invite. Treat it like a bill.
  2. Numbers open. Balances and upcoming bills. No surprises mid-meeting.
  3. Agenda on paper. Bills, goals, one worry each. Stick to it.
  4. No mind-reading. Say the fear. I am worried about the car beats sighing.
  5. One decision each meeting. Not twelve. Finish one change.
  6. Shared visibility. Joint view of pots even if accounts are separate.
  7. Use the pack. UK Payday Budget Pack gives a shared template.
