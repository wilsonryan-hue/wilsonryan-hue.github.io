How to chase unpaid invoices in the UK — 7 calm steps

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Most late invoices are not malice. They are lost in someone else’s inbox. Your job is a clear paper trail and a polite escalation.

Seven steps. Stay calm. Keep records.

  1. Check the invoice. Correct company name, PO if required, bank details, due date, your contact.
  2. Day after due. One short email: number, amount, due date, PDF attached again. No essay.
  3. Three days later. Same thread — confirm receipt, ask if anything is blocking payment.
  4. One week late. Phone the AP contact if you have one. Note the name and time.
  5. Two weeks late. Formal chase: overdue amount, original due date, date you pause new work if unpaid.
  6. Waiting-on list. Who owes what, since when, last contact. Memory is not a ledger.
  7. Ready prompts. If you want chase emails and a waiting-on sheet ready to paste, use the Freelancer pack.
