How to start a debt snowball in the UK — smallest balance first

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Debt snowball works because wins are visible. Smallest balance first, not lowest APR theatre.

Eight moves. List, then attack.

  1. List every debt. Balance, minimum, rate. Credit cards, overdraft, catalogue, family loans.
  2. Order by balance ascending. Snowball order. Avalanche is the rate order — pick one method.
  3. Minimums on all. Never skip a minimum to speed another. Fees hurt.
  4. Extra to the smallest. Every spare pound after bills.
  5. Celebrate the kill. Cross it off. Move that payment to the next.
  6. No new consumer credit. Freeze the cards in a block of ice if you must.
  7. Payday review. Recalculate after each payday.
  8. Printable pack. Debt Snowball Payoff Pack is this system as a PDF.
