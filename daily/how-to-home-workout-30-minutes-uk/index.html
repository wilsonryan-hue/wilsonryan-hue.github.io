How to do a 30-minute home workout — UK no-gym plan

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Home workouts fail when the plan needs kit you do not own. Bodyweight and a timer.

Seven moves. Living room floor.

  1. Clear 2 by 2 metres. Rug edge to sofa. That is the gym.
  2. Timer 30:00. Phone on loud. No playlist hunting mid-set.
  3. Five moves. Squats, press-ups (knees ok), lunges, plank, glute bridge.
  4. 40 on / 20 off. Six rounds. Done is the metric.
  5. Same time daily. After school drop-off or before tea. Habit slot.
  6. Progress one thing. Extra rep or slower tempo. Not a new programme every Monday.
  7. Reset the room. Mat away. Link to Sunday Reset so the house stays usable.
