How to plan revision before mocks — UK two-week sprint

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Mocks punish last-minute cramming. Two weeks of past papers beats a panic weekend.

Eight moves. Fourteen days.

  1. Mock timetable printed. Know the order. Prioritise earliest exams.
  2. One past paper per subject early. Baseline score. No shock on the day.
  3. Daily non-negotiable. 90 minutes after school. Written on the fridge.
  4. Weak topic mornings. Weekends: hardest topics when fresh.
  5. Mark and fix same day. Do not stockpile unmarked papers.
  6. Sleep and food. Mocks are endurance. Empty tank fails smart kids.
  7. No new content day before. Light review only. Trust the work.
  8. Sunday sprint plan. Pair this with the Revision Timetable so subjects get booked hours.
