How to build a university revision timetable — UK

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Uni revision fails when it ignores labs, shifts, and commuting. Build the timetable around the real week.

Eight steps for a degree revision week you can keep.

  1. List modules. Exam or coursework weight — circle the two that matter most this term.
  2. Map fixed time. Lectures, seminars, shifts, commute. Shade them out first.
  3. Block deep work. 90–120 minute blocks for hard topics. Morning if that is when you think.
  4. Weight topics. More blocks for weak modules. Do not give equal time to everything.
  5. Active methods. Past papers, flashcards, teaching aloud — not re-reading notes for hours.
  6. One weekly review. Sunday 30 minutes: what stuck, what moves forward.
  7. Protect sleep. No heroic all-nighters in the plan. Tired revision is theatre.
  8. Printable. A Revision Timetable gives undated weekly blocks and session lengths on one PDF.
