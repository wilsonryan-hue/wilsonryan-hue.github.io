How to build a 3-day gym routine — UK busy week plan

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Six-day splits fail busy people. Three solid sessions beat five half ones.

Eight moves. Three sessions. Done.

  1. Book the classes or slots. Calendar blocks. Treat like meetings.
  2. Push / pull / legs — or full body. Pick one split. Stick 8 weeks.
  3. 45-minute cap. Warm-up 5, work 35, out. No phone scrolling between sets.
  4. Compound first. Squats and presses before curls and gadgets.
  5. Commute gym if possible. Friction of a special trip kills consistency.
  6. Bag for the week. Same gym bag. Wash Sunday.
  7. Deload every 6th week. Lighter. Ego stays intact.
  8. Admin around it. Life Admin Checklist clears the excuses that steal gym slots.
