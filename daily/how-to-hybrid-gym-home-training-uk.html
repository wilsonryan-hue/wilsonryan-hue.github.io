How to mix gym and home training in one week — UK

24 September 2026 · 5 min

You do not need five gym trips. Pair two gym sessions with home work and keep one plan.

Eight moves for a hybrid week that holds together.

  1. Choose the split. Gym for heavy lifts or machines. Home for easy cardio, core, or short finishers.
  2. Book gym slots. Two fixed gym windows. Everything else is home — no daily travel guilt.
  3. Match volume. Do not double-leg day. One hard lower, one hard upper, then lighter home work.
  4. Home kit list. Mat, one pair of dumbbells or resistance band, timer. Nothing fancy.
  5. Same warm-up rule. Five minutes mobility before every session — gym or living room.
  6. Travel buffer. Gym days include drive/park time in the time-box so you are not late and rushed.
  7. One log. Write gym and home ticks on the same weekly grid so the week reads as one plan.
  8. Printable. Weekly Training Plan lets you label session types across gym and home on one page.
