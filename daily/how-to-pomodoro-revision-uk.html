How to use Pomodoro for revision — UK student method

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Pomodoro fails when breaks become scrolling. Protect the break rules.

Seven moves. 25 minutes that count.

  1. One topic per pomodoro. Not revise Science. Rate equations.
  2. 25 / 5 strict. Timer visible. Stand up on the break.
  3. Break equals no phone. Water, stretch, window. Apps kill the next block.
  4. Four then long break. 15 to 20 minutes. Snack. Then back.
  5. Track completed blocks. Tally marks. Motivation from evidence.
  6. Hard subjects first. Morning or after tea peak. Easy later.
  7. Sunday batch plan. Pair this with the Revision Timetable so subjects get booked hours.
