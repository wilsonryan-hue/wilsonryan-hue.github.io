How to start strength training as a beginner — UK home or gym

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Beginners fail by copying advanced splits. Full-body three days wins.

Eight moves. Three days a week.

  1. Learn five lifts. Squat pattern, hinge, push, pull, carry. Names optional; form not.
  2. Two sets to start. Leave two reps in the tank. Soreness is not a badge.
  3. Same days weekly. Mon, Wed, Fri or Tue, Thu, Sat. Protect the slots.
  4. Log the weight. Notebook. Next session adds a little if last felt solid.
  5. Protein at meals. Eggs, yoghurt, chicken, beans. Not a powder obsession.
  6. Sleep before volume. Add weight only when sleep is decent.
  7. Form video once. Film a set monthly. Fix one cue.
  8. Sunday plan. Put sessions in the Sunday Reset Workbook calendar block.
