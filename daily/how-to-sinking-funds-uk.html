How to set up sinking funds — UK household method

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Sinking funds turn annual shocks into weekly boredom. That is the point.

Eight moves. Predictable pots.

  1. List the shocks. Christmas, car MOT, holidays, kids' birthdays, boiler service.
  2. Annual cost divided by months. £600 Christmas / 12 = £50 monthly.
  3. Pots or envelopes. Bank pots named. Not one vague savings.
  4. Payday fill. Same morning as rent. Non-negotiable.
  5. Spend only from the pot. Christmas comes from Christmas pot. Not the overdraft.
  6. Review twice yearly. Costs change. Adjust.
  7. Start small. £10 pots beat perfect maths you never fund.
  8. Payday pack. UK Payday Budget Pack includes sinking fund lines.
