How to plan five teas under £10 — UK budget dinners

24 September 2026 · 5 min

Cheap teas fail when you buy ingredients for five different cuisines. Share the cupboard.

Eight moves. One shop. Five nights.

  1. Pick a protein lane. Mince or chicken thighs for the week. Not both fancy and cheap.
  2. Staples first. Rice, pasta, wraps, tinned tomatoes, frozen veg. Price-check own brand.
  3. Five recipes only. Chilli, pasta bake, stir-fry, jackets, omelette night. Write them.
  4. Portion maths. Weigh once. £10 includes oil and spice you already own.
  5. Same veg, three ways. Carrots and onions stretch across nights without looking identical.
  6. No midweek top-up. If it is not on the list, it waits until Sunday.
  7. Cook time cap. Under 30 minutes active. Slow cookers count if you set them before work.
  8. Print the pack. School-Night Teas Under 30 is this plan as a working PDF.
