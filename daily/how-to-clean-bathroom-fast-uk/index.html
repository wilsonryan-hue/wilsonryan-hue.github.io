How to clean your bathroom fast — UK 20-minute list

24 September 2026 · 5 min

A bathroom does not need a deep clean every day. It needs a fixed order so you finish before the water goes cold.

Eight steps. One pass. Do them in order.

  1. Clear surfaces. Bin tissues, empty the cup, products into one caddy. You cannot wipe clutter.
  2. Spray and wait. Sink, taps, toilet rim. Let chemistry work while you move.
  3. Mirror. Damp microfibre then dry. Streaks are usually too much product.
  4. Toilet. Bowl, seat, handle. Flush. That is the germ line.
  5. Sink and taps. Wipe with the spray you left sitting. Dry the taps.
  6. Floor pass. Wet wipe or mop pad around the base of the toilet and sink.
  7. Towels. Hang so they dry. Damp towels make damp rooms.
  8. Done. If school nights are chaos too, keep dinner as short as this clean.
