How to do the 52-week savings challenge — UK version

24 September 2026 · 5 min

The classic £1 to £52 ladder fails tight months. Use a flat weekly amount you can actually keep.

Seven moves. Fifty-two weeks.

  1. Pick a weekly amount. £5 or £10 beats a heroic ladder you abandon in week 12.
  2. Separate pot. Bank pot or jar. Not the spending account.
  3. Payday automation. Standing order the day after payday.
  4. Missed week rule. Double next week only if it does not break groceries. Else skip and continue.
  5. Track on paper. Ticks matter more than apps you ignore.
  6. Name the goal. Emergency buffer, Christmas, boiler. Vague savings die.
  7. Printable challenge. 52-Week Savings Challenge UK is the tracker PDF.
