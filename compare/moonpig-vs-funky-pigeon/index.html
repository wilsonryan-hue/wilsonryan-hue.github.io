Where they beat us

We would rather tell you now than have you find out later. Our comparison uses Moonpig's standard range: Moonpig also sells a value range from £2.99 before postage (about £4.89 with 1st class post, checked 21 October 2026), and both retailers often run first-order discounts. Our card is a little smaller than a Moonpig standard card and smaller than Funky Pigeon's A5.
