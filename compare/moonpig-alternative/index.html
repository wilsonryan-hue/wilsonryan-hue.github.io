Why people look for an alternative

The card price looks the same, but postage is added at the end of checkout. Across a year of birthdays that £1.90 adds up: ten cards cost £58.90 at Moonpig with 1st class post and £39.90 with us.

Where they beat us

We would rather tell you now than have you find out later. Our comparison uses Moonpig's standard range: Moonpig also sells a value range from £2.99 before postage (about £4.89 with 1st class post, checked 21 October 2026), and both retailers often run first-order discounts. Our card is a little smaller than a Moonpig standard card and smaller than Funky Pigeon's A5. We post at 2nd class speed, so if the birthday is tomorrow, Moonpig or Funky Pigeon next-day delivery is the better choice, at a higher price. They also have far more designs, licensed character ranges, gifts and flowers, and years of customer reviews. We are new and have no reviews yet.
