When Thortful is the better pick

Thortful is a marketplace of independent creators, so it has far more designs than we do, plus photo cards, face-swap cards, A4 cards, letterbox gifts and tracked next-day post. If you send a lot of cards, its XTRAthortful membership (£9.99 a year, 33% off cards) can beat our price per card once postage is added. If you want one thick matte card with the post already in the price, we are £1.90 cheaper.

Our card is smaller than Thortful's A5, and we post at 2nd class speed. We are new and have no reviews yet.
