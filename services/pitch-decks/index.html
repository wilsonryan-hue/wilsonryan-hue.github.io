How it works

  1. Email us what the deck is for and attach whatever you have: notes, an old deck, a business plan, figures, your logo.
  2. Pay the Stripe link we send.
  3. We send a one-page slide-by-slide outline first, so you can check the story before we design.
  4. You get the finished deck as PowerPoint, Google Slides and PDF, plus the changes rounds in your tier.

Questions

Will you make up figures or market sizes?
Never. Numbers come from you, or from a named public source we link on the slide. If a slide needs a figure nobody has, we tell you rather than guess.
Can you present it or join the call?
No, we work by email only. We can add speaker notes to every slide so you know what to say.
Do you use AI?
We use AI tools to draft and lay out faster. Every slide is read and checked by a person, and if you'd rather we didn't use AI, tell us and we won't.
Is this financial or investment advice?
No. We present your plan clearly; the figures and the decision are yours. For a loan or investment, an accountant should check the numbers.
Can you do a deck for my coursework?
No. We don't do assessed student work.
What if I'm not happy?
Tell us what's wrong and we'll fix it within your rounds. If we haven't started yet, we'll refund you in full. See our refund policy.
