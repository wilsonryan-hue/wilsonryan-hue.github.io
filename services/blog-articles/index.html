How it works

  1. Email us your website and the topic, or ask us to suggest three topics based on questions real customers ask about your trade.
  2. Pay the Stripe link we send. Then tell us anything we must include or avoid: prices, area, what makes you different, and how you like to sound.

Questions

Will this get me to the top of Google?
No honest writer can promise that, and you should be wary of anyone who does. What a good article does is answer a real question well, which is what search engines and customers both look for. Most small sites see results build over months, not days.
Will you make up facts or statistics?
No. We only use facts you give us or that come from a public source we can name. No invented figures, quotes, reviews or awards.
Do you use AI?
We use AI tools to help research and draft, and every article is rewritten and checked by hand before you see it. If you'd rather we didn't use AI at all, say so and we won't.
Who owns the article?
You do, once it's paid for. Publish it under your name or your business's name.
Are there topics you won't write?
We don't write medical, legal or financial advice that a qualified person should sign off, school or university work to be handed in for marks, or fake reviews.
What if I'm not happy?
Tell us what isn't right and we'll rework it within your included changes. If we haven't started yet, we'll refund you in full. See our refund policy.
