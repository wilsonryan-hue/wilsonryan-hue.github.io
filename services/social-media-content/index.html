How it works

  1. Email us a few lines about your business, your page link and anything coming up. Photos help but aren't essential.
  2. Pay the Stripe link we send.
  3. We read your page and website, then write the posts in your voice, not ours.

Questions

Do you post for me?
On the Every month plan we can, inside your own Meta Business Suite, if you add us as a helper. We never ask for your password.
Will you reply to comments and messages?
No, that stays with you, because your customers want to hear from you. We write posts that get people commenting, and you reply.
Will you make up reviews or offers?
Never. Prices, offers and reviews come from you. If a post needs something we don't know, we leave a clear gap and ask.
Do you do TikTok and Reels?
We can write Reel ideas and captions, but we don't film or appear on camera.
Do you use AI?
We use AI tools to draft faster. Every post is read and checked by a person, and if you'd rather we didn't use AI, tell us and we won't.
What if I'm not happy?
Tell us what's wrong and we'll fix it within your rounds. If we haven't started yet, we'll refund you in full. See our refund policy.
