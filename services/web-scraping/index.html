What people use it for

  • Shop owners: a competitor's prices and stock in one sheet, to price your own range.
  • Agencies and sales teams: a public directory or member list turned into a prospect sheet (business details only).
  • Moving shop platform: your own old product catalogue pulled off your site, ready to import into Shopify or WooCommerce.
  • Researchers and buyers: events, listings, tenders or job ads gathered from several sites into one place.

How it works

  1. Pick a package and pay securely through Stripe. Type the website address and the data you need at checkout.
  2. Email us any extra detail: the exact columns, an example page, filters (for example "only items under £50").
  3. We collect the data, check a sample against the live site by hand, and remove duplicates.
  4. You get an Excel file and a CSV by email, with a short note on anything we couldn't read.

Questions

What can't you collect?
Anything behind a login or paywall, anything a site's terms or robots rules forbid, and personal data about private individuals (home addresses, personal emails or phone numbers). Business details that a company publishes itself are fine. If your site is a no-go, we'll tell you before we start and refund you in full.
What if the site has fewer rows than I paid for?
You get everything that's there. If it's under half your package, we'll refund the difference or move you to the smaller package.
Can it update every week?
Yes. Email for a fixed monthly price.
Do you use AI?
The collecting is done with our own code, and we use AI tools to help tidy messy text. Every file is checked against the live site before it's sent.
What if it's wrong?
Tell us which rows and we'll fix them free. See our refund policy.
