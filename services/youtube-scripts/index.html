How it works

  1. Email us with the details above. Rough is fine; we'll ask if anything's missing.
  2. Pay the Stripe link we send.
  3. We research the topic, write the script in your voice and list the sources.
  4. You get a Word document (and a Google Doc if you like) ready to record from. We change anything you're not happy with.

What we won't do

  • We don't copy or "rewrite" other creators' videos. Every script is researched and written from scratch.
  • No made-up facts, quotes or statistics. If we can't find a source for something, it doesn't go in.
  • No fake drama, misleading thumbnails or medical and money advice dressed up as fact.
  • We write the words. We don't record voiceovers, edit video or upload to your channel.

Questions

Which niches do you write for?
History, true stories, science explainers, travel, business stories, nature, top-10 and "how it works" channels all suit us well. Ask if yours is different.
Will YouTube treat it as AI content?
YouTube cares about original, valuable videos, not how the script was typed. Ours are researched, sourced and written for your channel, not mass-produced. If you use a synthetic voice, YouTube asks you to label realistic altered content, and we'll remind you when it applies.
Do you use AI?
We use AI tools to research and draft faster. Every script is fact-checked and edited by a person, and if you'd rather we didn't use AI, tell us and we won't.
Can you do Shorts?
Yes. Ask for a quote, or add three Shorts scripts cut from your long video to any order.
What if I'm not happy?
Tell us what's wrong and we'll fix it in your round of changes. If we haven't started yet, we'll refund you in full. See our refund policy.
