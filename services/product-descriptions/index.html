Questions

Which platforms is it for?
Any. The spreadsheet has a column per field, so you can paste into Shopify, Etsy, eBay, Amazon, WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace or Not On The High Street, or import it as a CSV.
Will it help with Google?
Each product gets a meta title and description written around what people actually search for. We don't promise rankings, because nobody honestly can, but clear unique copy beats duplicated supplier text.
Do you use AI?
We use AI tools to help draft, and every description is checked by hand against your product facts. If you'd rather we didn't use AI at all, say so and we won't.
What won't you write?
Medical or health claims, supplements, vapes, CBD, weapons, adult products, or anything that copies another brand's wording.
What if I'm not happy?
Reply with what you'd like changed and we'll revise it. If we haven't started yet, we'll refund you in full. See our refund policy.
