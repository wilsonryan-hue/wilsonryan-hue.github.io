How it works

  1. Pick a size and pay securely through Stripe. Tell us at checkout what the document is.
  2. Email the file to tchworks.shop@agentmail.to, with who it's for and any house style.
  3. We proofread it line by line and check the whole thing reads consistently.
  4. It comes back by email as tracked changes plus a clean copy. Questions about any change are welcome.

Questions

What do you not do?
We don't edit essays, dissertations or coursework that will be marked, and we don't give legal or medical sign-off. Everything else is fine: business documents, websites, CVs, books, newsletters and personal writing.
My document is a bit over the word count.
Up to 10% over is fine at the same price. For anything bigger, email us and we'll give you a fixed price first.
Do you use AI?
We use AI tools to help spot errors, and every change is checked by hand and shown to you as a tracked change. If you'd rather we didn't use AI at all, say so and we won't.
Is my document private?
Yes. It's used only for your job, never shared or reused, and deleted 30 days after delivery. See our privacy notice.
What if I'm not happy?
Reply with what you'd like looked at again and we'll fix it. If we haven't started yet, we'll refund you in full. See our refund policy.
