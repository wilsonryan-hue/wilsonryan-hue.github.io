How it works

  1. Pick a package and pay securely through Stripe. Tell us your website address and which pages at checkout.
  2. Answer six short questions by email (who your customers are, what you're best at, prices, area, how people should contact you, and anything to avoid).
  3. We rewrite each page, check it reads well on a phone, and add a page title and Google description.
  4. You get a Word document and a Google Doc, laid out heading by heading, ready to paste into Wix, Squarespace, WordPress or Shopify.

Questions

Do I need a website already?
It helps, but no. If you're starting from scratch, tell us about the business in the email and we'll write the pages from your answers.
Will you make things up?
No. We only use facts you give us or that are already on your site. No invented reviews, awards, prices or promises.
Do you put it on the website for me?
The price covers the writing. You paste it in yourself, which takes most people ten minutes per page. If you'd like us to do it, ask for a quote.
Do you use AI?
We use AI tools to help draft, and every line is rewritten and checked by hand against your answers. If you'd rather we didn't use AI at all, say so and we won't.
What if I'm not happy?
Tell us what isn't right and we'll rework it within your included changes. If we haven't started yet, we'll refund you in full. See our refund policy.
