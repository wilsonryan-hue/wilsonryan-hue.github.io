Extract: "What need will your project address?"

Demand has outgrown what we can store. In the 12 months to September our sign-in sheets recorded 1,312 visits from 186 households. On 31 of our 98 sessions we turned food away at collection because we had nowhere cold to keep it, and on 12 sessions we ran out of chilled food before the last members arrived.

We are not asking you to fund food. The food is already there. We are asking you to fund the means to store it, share it and show people how to cook it cheaply.

How it works

  1. Pay through Stripe for the short application, or email to book the full one. Tell us the funder and your organisation's name.
  2. Answer our questionnaire by email and send the link to the fund's guidance or form questions.
  3. We write every answer to the funder's word limits, using only your facts and figures, and flag anything the funder asks for that you haven't given us.
  4. You check it, we make your changes, and you submit it yourself.

Questions

Can you guarantee we'll get the grant?
No one honestly can. Funders turn down good bids when a fund is oversubscribed. What we promise is a clear application that answers every question, fits the criteria and is in on time.
Do you use AI?
AI helps with first drafts. Every answer is then rewritten and checked by us against your facts and the funder's guidance. Some funders ask whether AI was used; we'll tell you exactly how it was, so you can answer honestly.
Will you make up statistics?
Never. We only use figures you give us or public sources we name. If an answer needs evidence you don't have, we'll tell you what to collect.
Do you submit it for us?
No, the application stays yours: you submit it from your own account and sign any declarations.
How close to the deadline can we leave it?
Allow at least 3 working days for a short application and 5 for a full one, after your answers arrive. Tight deadline? Email us first.
What if we're not happy?
Tell us what to change and we'll rewrite it. See our refund policy.
