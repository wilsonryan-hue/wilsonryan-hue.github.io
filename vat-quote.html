VAT on quotes UK

If you are VAT registered, the quote should show net, VAT and gross. If you are not, do not add 20%.

The quote is the price they will pay

A quote that says £2,000 and an invoice that says £2,400 is how jobs turn into arguments. If you are VAT registered, write net, the rate, VAT, and the gross.

If you are not VAT registered, do not add a VAT line because a template had a box. Write the amount due.
