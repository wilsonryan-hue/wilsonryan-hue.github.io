The address you enter at checkout is where the card goes. To send it to the birthday person, enter their address. To write it yourself, enter your own and leave the message empty.

Changes and cancellations

Each card is printed to order with your message, so it is a personalised item. Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations, personalised goods are not covered by the 14-day cancellation right. If you spot a typo or a wrong address, email hello@tchworks.co.uk straight away. If we catch it before printing starts we will fix it or cancel and refund you.

Returns and refunds

We don't take returns of personalised cards that arrive as ordered. Your rights under the Consumer Rights Act are not affected. If your card:

  • arrives damaged,
  • has a print fault, or isn't the design or message you ordered, or
  • hasn't arrived 10 working days after you ordered,

email hello@tchworks.co.uk within 30 days of ordering with your order email (and a photo if it's damaged). We will reprint and resend it free, or refund the full amount including delivery to your original payment method, whichever you prefer. You won't need to send anything back.
