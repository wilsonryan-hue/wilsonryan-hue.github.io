Delivery and returns

Price: £3.99 per card including UK delivery. No postage line added at checkout.

Printing: made to order on 324gsm Mohawk Superfine matte card, 7x5 inches, with a kraft envelope, printed in the UK.

Delivery: printed within 1 to 3 working days, then Royal Mail standard (2nd class speed). Allow 3 to 6 working days in total. UK addresses only for now. No next-day option yet.

Something wrong? If the card arrives damaged, misprinted or does not arrive within 10 working days, email tchworks.shop@agentmail.to and we reprint it free or refund you in full. Personalised cards cannot be returned for a change of mind, which is allowed under UK consumer law for made-to-order items.

Your data: we use the address only to post the card. Uploaded photos are deleted 30 days after printing.
