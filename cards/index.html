How it works

  1. Pick a design. 288 originals, made by us, not copied from anyone.
  2. Make it theirs. Edit the front, choose from 8 fonts and 12 colourways, upload a photo, write your message.
  3. Pay £3.99. Card and UK delivery in one price, by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Stripe.
  4. We post it. Printed within 1 to 3 working days and sent by Royal Mail in a kraft envelope.

Questions

Why is it cheaper?

We print to order with a UK print partner and do not run TV adverts. You pay one price with no postage line added at the end.

How quickly will it arrive?

We print within 1 to 3 working days. Then choose Royal Mail 2nd class (from £3.99 all-in), 1st class (from £4.79) or Tracked 24 after printing (from £8.49, 1-3 days print first). We do not offer same-day dispatch yet, so order a few days ahead. Full delivery details.

Can I send several cards?

Yes. Add as many as you like to your basket. Each payment goes to one UK address, so cards for different people are paid separately.

Is the card the same size as Moonpig?

Ours is 7x5 inches (178x127mm), a little smaller than a Moonpig Standard (185x132mm) and an A5. It is printed on 324gsm Mohawk Superfine, which is thicker than the 250 to 300gsm Moonpig lists.

Can I send it to myself?

Yes. Put your own address at checkout and leave the inside message blank if you want to write it yourself.
