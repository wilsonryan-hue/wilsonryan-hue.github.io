Questions

Is it really cheaper?

Yes, on list prices checked 19 October 2026: one standard birthday card plus the cheapest UK delivery offered. Moonpig came to £5.89. Ours is £4.58. Promo codes and multi-buys can change their price, and prices change, so check on the day.

What is different?

Their cheapest post is 1st Class. Ours goes Royal Mail at 2nd Class speed after printing, so allow 2-6 working days in total. Our card is 7 x 5 in (127 x 178 mm) on 324gsm matt fine-art card with a kraft envelope.

Can I add my own message?

Yes. Type it at checkout and we print it inside, or leave it blank and post it to yourself to write by hand.
