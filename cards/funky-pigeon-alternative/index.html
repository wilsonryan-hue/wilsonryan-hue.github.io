Where Funky Pigeon is the better choice

  • You want a bigger or glossy card. Funky Pigeon is A5 or larger (A4 and A3 too) on 330gsm gloss; ours is 7 x 5 in on matte.
  • You want photo collages. They have multi-photo templates. We take one photo.
  • You are sending a batch. Funky Pigeon takes 50% off 5 or more cards and has an address book. We have neither.
  • You need it fast. They have express options and a posted-today cut-off. We post at 2nd class speed by default.
  • You trust a known name. They have years of reviews. We are new and have none yet.
