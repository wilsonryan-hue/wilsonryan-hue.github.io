Questions

What about half-term and INSET days?

Term dates vary across the UK, so the questions run Monday to Friday. On a day off, just skip it.

I have one child at primary and one at secondary.

Download both versions. Same price, it's one purchase.

Can I change the time?

Yes. The download page lets you choose a different time, for example if your child walks home, and builds your file straight away.

What if my child tells me something worrying?

Thank them for telling you, stay calm, and talk to their school. For concerns about a child's safety, the NSPCC helpline is 0808 800 5000.
