Week one, word for word

This is the 'for her' version. Versions for him and for them use the same prompts with the right words.

Day 1 · 7:30
Name one specific thingSend her a message today naming one specific thing she did this week that made your life easier. Not 'thanks for everything'. The actual thing: 'Thanks for sorting the car insurance. I know that was forty minutes on hold.'
Day 2 · 7:30
Turn towards the small stuffToday, every time she points something out (a headline, a dog in the street, a message from a friend) stop and respond properly. Look up, put the phone face down, ask one question about it.
Day 3 · 7:30
The first five minutesWhen you next see her today, give the first five minutes to her, not to logistics. Greet her properly, ask how the day went, listen. Who's picking up whom and what's for tea can wait five minutes.
Day 4 · 7:30
Do one thing from her listFind one job that has been sitting on her to-do list (the dentist booking, the parcel return, the form for school) and do it, start to finish, without being asked. Tell her once it's done, not before.
Day 5 · 7:30
Ask about the thingThink back to something she mentioned earlier this week: a meeting, a worry, a friend's news. Ask how it went, using the detail. 'How did the meeting with Sam go? Did they go for the new rota?'
Day 6 · 7:30
Make it exactly rightMake her a drink or a snack exactly the way she likes it, without asking how she takes it. If you're not sure, that's today's job: find out and remember.
Day 7 · 7:30
First weekly check-in (15 minutes)Find 15 calm minutes today. Ask two questions and only listen: 'What's one thing I did this week that you liked?' and 'What's one thing that would make next week easier for you?' Don't defend or explain. Say thanks, and pick one thing to act on.

Questions

Will my partner see it?

Only if they look at your calendar. The events are marked as free time, so they won't show you as busy.

Is this therapy or counselling?

No. These are everyday ideas for a healthy relationship. If things feel unsafe or overwhelming, please talk to your GP or a relationship counselling service.

Can I change the time of the alert?

Yes. The download page lets you pick a different time and start date and builds your file there and then.

Does it work on Android?

Yes, through Google Calendar. The download page has step-by-step instructions, including how to make sure the alerts pop up.
