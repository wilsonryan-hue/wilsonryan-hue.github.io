Questions

Do I need an app store?

No. It's a web app: open the link, add it to your home screen, and it behaves like an app, offline too.

Can I add my own recipes?

Yes. Type the ingredients one per line and they're added to your shopping list along with everything else.

How does the cheapest-shop feature work?

You type the prices you see, once, for the things you usually buy. One List remembers them and works out which of your shops is cheapest for this week's list. We don't pull prices from supermarket websites.

Is it suitable for allergies?

It's a planning aid. Recipes are tagged with the 14 main allergens and you can hide any you avoid, but ingredients vary by brand, so always check labels. Take extra care with severe allergies.

Is it a subscription?

No. £4.99 once.
