Questions

Do I need to download anything from the App Store or Google Play?

No. Fair Rota is a web app. You open the link, add it to your home screen, and it works like an app, including offline.

Can the whole family use it?

It's designed to sit on one phone or a shared tablet that everyone can see. Your rota is stored on that device. You can share the week's rota to your family chat in one tap.

Is my information private?

Yes. Names and chores are saved only on your device. There's no account and nothing is sent to us.

What ages does it work for?

From about age 4 up. Each chore has a youngest age group, so a 5-year-old gets 'lay the table', not 'clean the bathroom'.

What if I change phones?

Use Save backup in Setup, then Restore on the new phone.

Is it a subscription?

No. £3.99 once.
