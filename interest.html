Statutory interest on late payment UK

What statutory interest is, when people mention it, and why the first move is still a short page with the amount. Not legal advice.

The reminder comes first

Most late invoices are not a court case. They are a missing ask. Invoice number, amount, original due date, how to pay. Send that the day after it is due.

Statutory interest under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act is a separate decision. It is not the first letter. A template should not pretend to calculate it for you.

What this page will not do

It will not tell you that you are owed a specific rate. It will not draft a claim. It will not replace a solicitor. TCH Late Pay is the two-page reminder you actually send.
