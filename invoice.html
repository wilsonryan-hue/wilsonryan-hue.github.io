What a UK sole trader invoice must include

Unique number, your name, address for legal documents, the client, the work, the amount, VAT if registered. A page, not an app.

GOV.UK is short. Keep it short.

A unique identification number. Your name and any trading name. An address where legal documents can be delivered if you use a business name. The client. A description of the work. The amount. VAT details if you are registered. How to pay. When it is due.

Branding is optional. Software is optional. A numbered page you will send is not.
