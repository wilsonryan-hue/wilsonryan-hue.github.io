How to chase unpaid invoices (UK)

Four clear steps. Forty-five to seventy-five seconds. UK English voiceover.

Get the Freelancer AI Invoice pack Browse shop

Steps in this guide

  1. Send same-day — Invoice the day you deliver. Late paperwork invites late pay.
  2. Polite day-7 nudge — One short chase: amount, due date, and pay link. No drama.
  3. Day-14 escalate — Formal reminder. Quote Late Payment of Commercial Debts Act terms.
  4. Stop new work — Pause further deliveries until the balance clears. Protect your time.
