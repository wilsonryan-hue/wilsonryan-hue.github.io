How to budget on UK payday

Four clear steps. Forty-five to seventy-five seconds. UK English voiceover.

Steps in this guide

  1. Pay yourself first — Move savings and bills the morning money lands. Before you spend.
  2. Three pots only — Bills, spending, and buffer. Keep it simple so it sticks.
  3. Cap the fun money — Set a weekly cash figure. When it is gone, it is gone.
  4. Review next payday — Ten minutes. What leaked? Fix one leak before the next cycle.
