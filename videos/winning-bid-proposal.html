How to write a winning bid

Four clear steps. Forty-five to seventy-five seconds. UK English voiceover.

Steps in this guide

  1. Mirror their brief — Open with their goal in their words. Prove you listened.
  2. One clear outcome — State the result, timeline, and what done looks like. No fluff.
  3. Price with options — Good, better, best. Let them choose scope, not stall on yes or no.
  4. Close with next step — One action: call, deposit, or kickoff date. Make it easy to say yes.
