How to set up a site FAQ chatbot

Four clear steps. Forty-five to seventy-five seconds. UK English voiceover.

Get the Done-For-You Chatbot Browse shop

Steps in this guide

  1. List top ten asks — Pull the questions you answer every week. That is your FAQ seed.
  2. Write short answers — Two to four sentences each. Clear. No jargon. Link to the page.
  3. Embed on key pages — Home, pricing, and contact. Meet visitors where they stall.
  4. Review weekly hits — Check what people ask. Add gaps. Cut noise. Keep it useful.
