How to chase an invoice UK

Chase a late UK invoice without a paragraph. Number, amount, original due date, how to pay. Two pages. £12.

They have not forgotten

A late invoice is rarely a mystery. The client is waiting to see if you will ask again. A long email trains them that the ask is optional. A short page with the amount is the ask.

When to send it

The day after it is due. Then seven days. Then you stop work if you said you would. Do not write a term you will not keep.

What not to write

Do not apologise for asking. Do not retell the job. Do not offer a discount to make the awkwardness go away. If you want a payment plan, put that on a second line, not instead of the number.
