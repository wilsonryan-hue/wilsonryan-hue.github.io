Common questions

What is the last posting date for Christmas cards 2026 in the UK?

Royal Mail's published last posting dates for Christmas 2026 are Friday 18 December for 2nd Class, Saturday 19 December for Tracked 48 and Monday 21 December for 1st Class and Tracked 24. Royal Mail says dates can change and advises posting as early as possible.

Is it too late to post a Christmas card by 2nd class on 19 December?

On Royal Mail's published dates, yes: the last posting date for 2nd Class is Friday 18 December. After that, 1st Class is published until Monday 21 December, and Tracked 48 until Saturday 19 December.

Does Royal Mail deliver on Christmas Day, Boxing Day and 28 December 2026?

No. Royal Mail lists no collections or deliveries on Friday 25 December, Saturday 26 December or Monday 28 December 2026 (England and Wales and Northern Ireland). Services resume on Tuesday 29 December.

How many Christmas cards should I write each evening?

Count the cards on your list, choose the date you want them all posted by, and divide. The planner on this page does the sum for you and shows how many to write on each evening from today until that date.
