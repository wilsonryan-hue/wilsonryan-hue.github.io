Common questions

What do you put in a Christmas Eve box?

Most boxes hold a few cosy and calm things for the evening: new pyjamas or socks, a book, a hot chocolate treat and a small activity such as a puzzle, colouring or a card game. Some families add a Christmas film to watch together. This page builds a list by age and budget, and adds three items that cost nothing.

When do you open a Christmas Eve box?

Families usually open it on the evening of Christmas Eve, which is Thursday 24 December in 2026, before bed. Some open it earlier in the day. There is no rule, so pick what suits your house.

How much should I spend on a Christmas Eve box?

There is no right amount. Type in what you want to spend per child, including £0. The page splits that budget between pyjamas or socks, a book, a treat and an activity, and the split is our suggestion, so move money between items as you like.

What can I put in a Christmas Eve box for free?

A handwritten note, a choice of Christmas film and a planned bedtime story cost nothing. Add a drawing, a plan for a family game or a list of favourite memories from the year and the box is still worth opening.

Do I have to buy a box?

No. A shoebox, a gift bag, a wrapped cardboard box or a laundry basket all work, and children can decorate it. The box is not counted in the budget on this page.

Is anything saved or sent?

No. The list is built in your browser. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere, so print it before you close the page.
