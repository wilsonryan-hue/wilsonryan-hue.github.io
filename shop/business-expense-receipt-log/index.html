When do I get it?

By email, usually within a few hours and always within 12 hours. After payment we check it with Stripe and email your files, usually within a few hours and always within 12 hours. Stripe emails your receipt too. If anything goes wrong, email hello@tchworks.co.uk and we'll send it.

Can I fill it in on screen?

Yes. Open it in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free), Chrome, Edge or Mac Preview and click any line or box. On a phone, Adobe Acrobat Reader works best.

Can I print it more than once?

Yes, print as many copies as you need for your own household or business. Please don't share or resell the file.

What if something's wrong with the file?

Email us and we'll fix it or refund you.

How does the phone calendar work?

Your files come with a calendar (.ics) file you can open on iPhone, Android, Google Calendar, Outlook or Mac to add the reminders. The reminders repeat on their own. Remove the calendar any time.
