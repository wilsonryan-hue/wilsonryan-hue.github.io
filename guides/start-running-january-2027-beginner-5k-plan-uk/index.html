Start running in January 2027 (UK): a 9-week beginner 5K plan with dates

If "start running" is on the list for 2027, this is the whole thing laid out. Start on Monday 4 January 2027, run three times a week, and the last session of week 9 is Sunday 7 March: 30 minutes of running without stopping, which is about 5K for most beginners. Every session is dated below.

Why a walk-run plan works

Beginners usually stop because they go out too fast, get out of breath in two minutes and decide they are "not a runner". A walk-run plan fixes that by starting with 60-second runs and adding a little each week, so your heart, lungs and legs adapt together. Three sessions a week, with a rest day between, is enough. NHS guidelines say adults should aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity or 75 minutes of vigorous activity a week, plus strengthening on at least 2 days. Running counts as vigorous, so three 30-minute runs by March covers the aerobic part.

Five rules that get beginners to week 9

  1. Slow enough to talk. If you can't say a short sentence, slow down. Nobody is timing you.
  2. Repeat a week if it felt too hard. Weeks 5 to 7 are the big jumps. Repeating one is normal, not failure.
  3. Rest days are part of the plan. Never run two days in a row in the first nine weeks.
  4. Sharp pain is a stop sign. A dull ache is normal; a sharp or worsening pain isn't. Rest, and see a GP or physio if it lasts.
  5. Dark January evenings: wear something bright or reflective, run where it's lit, and tell someone your route.

Frequently asked questions

Can a complete beginner run 5K in 9 weeks?
Most people can follow a walk-run plan like this one: three short sessions a week, starting with 60-second runs and building to 30 minutes of running by week 9. Repeating a week when it feels too hard is normal and part of the plan. If you have a health condition or have not exercised for a long time, check with your GP first.
If I start on 4 January 2027, when do I finish?
Week 1 starts on Tuesday 5 January and the last run of week 9 is on Sunday 7 March 2027. A free parkrun 5K on Saturday 13 March would make a good first event.
How fast should I run?
Slowly enough to talk in short sentences. Running too fast is the main reason beginners find the early weeks impossible. Speed comes later; the plan is about time on your feet.
How much exercise do adults need?
NHS guidelines say adults should aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity or 75 minutes of vigorous activity a week, plus strengthening activities on at least 2 days a week. Running counts as vigorous activity.
What if I miss a run or a week?
Miss one run and just do the next one. Miss a week or more and repeat the last week you completed before moving on. The dates are a guide, not a test.
Is this the NHS Couch to 5K plan?
No. It is TCH Works' own nine-week walk-run plan. The NHS has its own free Couch to 5K app if you prefer audio coaching.
