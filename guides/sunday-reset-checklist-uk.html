Sunday reset checklist (90 minutes)

Monday mess starts on Sunday night. A full “life overhaul” is too big. This is a timed 90-minute pass so the week starts calm — kitchen, clothes, calendar, bags, then ten minutes of digital.

Get the free PDF: Sunday Reset magnet · or skip ahead to Life Admin Checklist · £4.99

The 90-minute fence

  1. Kitchen surfaces + sink (20)
  2. Clothes: wash / dry / fold or hamper (20)
  3. Calendar skim + three commitments (15)
  4. Bags, keys, chargers home (15)
  5. Digital: inbox + downloads clear (10)
  6. Stop. Soft fence. Do not restart.

What this is not

Not a four-hour purge. Not a new personality. Food and calendar before decorative tidying.
