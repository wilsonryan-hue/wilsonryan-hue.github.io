How much does a dog groom cost in London and the South East? (October 2026)

A full groom for a small dog costs between £45 and £80 at the 11 groomers we checked, with a middle price of £58. In London the middle starting price is about £66. Large dogs start at £75 to £112. Here's the full table, taken from each groomer's own website.

What else you might pay for

  • Nail clipping on its own: £15 at six of the seven groomers that list it, £12 at one.
  • Ultrasonic teeth cleaning: usually £30 a session, with a first session at £45 to £80.
  • Matting: expect a surcharge if your dog's coat is badly matted, for example £20 extra, or £12 per 15 minutes of de-matting.
  • Fluffy coats: one groomer that splits prices by coat (Dirty Dog Washes) charges £10 to £20 more for fluffy coats than smooth ones.

Why prices vary so much

Size and coat matter most: a small smooth-coated dog takes far less time than a large doodle. Location matters too: the two Kent groomers in our sample start at £45 and £55, while Fulham boutique salons start at £74 and £80 and include extras like fragrance and treats. Always ask what's included before comparing.

How we checked

We took every dog groomer in London, Essex, Hertfordshire, Kent and Suffolk listed with a website on OpenStreetMap (47 websites), read each one, and kept the 11 that publish a clear full-groom price. It's a small sample, so treat it as a guide. Download the full report (PDF) or the data (CSV).
