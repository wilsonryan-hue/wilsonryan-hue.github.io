Delivery and returns

Delivery

Every T-shirt and mug is printed to order by our UK print partner and posted direct to the address you give at checkout. Delivery to any UK address is free.

  • Printing: 2 to 4 working days
  • Royal Mail: usually 2 to 4 working days
  • Total: allow 4 to 8 working days. Order by 10 December for Christmas.

We email you when your order has been posted. We currently deliver to the UK only.

Faulty, damaged or misprinted

Email tchworks.shop@agentmail.to within 14 days of delivery with your order email and a photo. We will send a free replacement or a full refund, your choice. You do not need to send a broken mug back.

Changed your mind or wrong size

You can cancel within 14 days of delivery under the Consumer Contracts Regulations. Email us first, then post the unworn, unwashed item back within 14 days. We refund the item price within 14 days of getting it back. Return postage is paid by you unless the item was faulty or we sent the wrong thing.

Cancelling before it is printed

Email us within 2 hours of ordering and we will cancel and refund in full if it has not gone to print.
