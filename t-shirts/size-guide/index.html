T-shirt size guide

Our tees are the Bella+Canvas 3001 unisex retail fit: slimmer than a classic boxy tee. If you like a relaxed fit, or are between sizes, go one size up. Measurements are of the garment laid flat and can vary by about 2.5 cm (1 inch).

How to measure: lay a tee you like flat, measure straight across 2.5 cm below the armpits (chest width), and from the highest point of the shoulder to the hem (length). Pick the size closest to it.
