Size guide

T-shirts (Bella+Canvas 3001)

Unisex retail fit, slimmer than a classic boxy tee. Between sizes, or like it relaxed? Go one size up. Garment measured flat; allow about 2.5 cm (1 inch) either way.

Hoodies (AWDis JH001) and sweatshirts (AWDis JH030)

Regular unisex fit. Chest to fit (inches): XS 34, S 36, M 40, L 44, XL 48, 2XL 52, 3XL 56 (sweatshirts S to 2XL).

How to measure: lay a top you like flat and measure straight across 2.5 cm below the armpits, then from the highest point of the shoulder to the hem.
